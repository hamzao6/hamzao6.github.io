About

Designer first, builder too.

I'm a graphic designer working mainly in Canva and Adobe Illustrator — logos, brand identity, social content, and print. I care about the details most people scroll past: the kerning, the grid, the two millimeters of extra padding that make something feel considered instead of assembled.

I also build the websites that show the work off, which means the design itself never gets lost in translation between the file and the browser.
